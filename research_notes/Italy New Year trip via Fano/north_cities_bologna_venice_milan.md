# Northern Italian cities for 26 Dec 2026 – 2 Jan 2027 (incl. New Year's Eve): Bologna, Venice, Milan, Verona, plus Ferrara/Modena/Parma/Ravenna add-ons

Research context: trip starts in Bologna (UAE flight in) and includes Fano (Marche coast). Researched 9 Nov 2026. Most 2026–27 holiday programmes are not yet published; where a fact is from the 2025–26 edition it is labelled **(last year)**. Italian cities usually publish their Capodanno programmes from early to mid December (the Bologna Welcome NYE page found was last updated on 29 Dec 2025; Sky TG24's Milan NYE round-up was 22 Dec 2025; Ferrara's programme article was 19 Dec 2025). Method note: page fetches were blocked in this session (bolognawelcome.com and pixidia.com both failed to resolve), so nearly everything below comes from search-result summaries of the cited pages and has not been read in full; treat exact times and prices as "verify". 26 web searches were used.

Calendar for the week (computed): Sat 26 Dec (Santo Stefano, public holiday) · Sun 27 · **Mon 28 (Last Supper closed on Mondays)** · Tue 29 · Wed 30 · Thu 31 Dec (NYE) · Fri 1 Jan (public holiday, heavy closures) · Sat 2 Jan.

## 1. Verdict and score out of 10 for each city for THIS week (and for NYE specifically)

### Takeaway
For a trip that starts in Bologna and goes to Fano, the best plan is **Bologna for New Year's Eve, plus Venice as the one extra northern city** (2 nights, either 29–31 Dec or 1–2 Jan). Bologna's free "Rogo del Vecchione" in Piazza Maggiore is the most distinctive and least stressful NYE of the group. Venice is the most atmospheric city for the week, and no access fee is due on these dates. Milan is the weakest choice for this specific week: no Piazza Duomo concert for six years running, a restricted-access square, and Last Supper tickets almost certainly gone. Its main pluses are shopping (winter sales should open about 2 Jan) and La Scala's *Otello* on 29 Dec or 2 Jan. Verona is a good, compact alternative for NYE, with a free concert and fireworks at the Arena. Its own market closes around 26–28 Dec.

### Cited Findings
- Bologna NYE (last year): free party in Piazza Maggiore from 23:00, music, lights and DJ sets, then the burning of an artist-designed "Vecchione" at midnight (2025–26: a "Gremlin oviparo" by DEM/Marco Barbieri) and a nationally known DJ after midnight. The modern rogo tradition dates from 1922 — [Bologna Welcome NYE blog](https://www.bolognawelcome.com/en/blog/cosa-fare-a-capodanno-a-bologna); [VoloScontato Bologna NYE 2026](https://www.voloscontato.it/sagre-ed-eventi/tutto-sul-capodanno-2026-a-bologna-info-dove-e-programma.php); [Il Resto del Carlino](https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/bologna/cosa-fare/capodanno-piazza-maggiore-igh7zh84)
- Venice NYE (last year): free fireworks over St Mark's Basin, with security checks at controlled viewing points. Official advice was that the fireworks are **not visible from Piazza or Piazzetta San Marco**; the best view is near the ACTV Arsenale stop on the Riva degli Schiavoni — [Venezia Unica](https://www.veneziaunica.it/en/node/2396); [Idealista](https://www.idealista.it/news/node/194239); [Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/lifestyle/2025/12/09/mete-capodanno-2026-viaggio)
- Milan NYE (last year): no free concert in Piazza Duomo, the sixth year in a row. The Olympic "Albero dei Giochi" stood in the square instead, and access was capped — [MilanoFree](https://www.milanofree.it/milano/eventi/capodanno-2026-in-piazza-duomo-a-milano-niente-concerto-albero-olimpico-e-cosa-fare-in-citta.html); [MenteLocale](https://www.mentelocale.it/milano/101469-capodanno-2026-in-piazza-duomo-a-milano-ancora-nessun-concerto-info-e-motivi.htm)
- Verona NYE (last year): free concert in Piazza Bra in front of the Arena (RTL 102.5 with the Comune and Fondazione Arena), fireworks at midnight over the Arena, then a party until 03:00 — [SiViaggia Verona NYE](https://siviaggia.it/idee-di-viaggio/capodanno-verona-cosa-fare/506559/); [Money.it NYE concerts](https://www.money.it/concerti-capodanno-2026-chi-canta-il-31-dicembre-nelle-principali-piazze-italiane)
- Venice access fee: the 2027 calendar is reported as 26 Mar–15 Aug 2027 (72 days), so no late-December or early-January dates — [Pixidia](https://pixidia.com/?p=92545)
- Milan: Last Supper is closed on Mondays, 25 Dec and 1 Jan, and the late-December inventory was released months ago (Section 3) — [VoloScontato Cenacolo](https://www.voloscontato.it/sagre-ed-eventi/museo-del-cenacolo-vinciano-come-comprare-i-biglietti-costo-riduzioni-orari.php)

## 2. Christmas markets and lights still running after 26 December

### Takeaway
By 27 Dec many classic markets have closed: Bologna's Fiera di Santa Lucia (26–27 Dec) and Verona's Nuremberg-style market (26–28 Dec). Bologna's Via Altabella fair and Milan's Duomo market run to about 6 Jan. Bologna's art lights run into March, so the festive look survives the whole week. Bolzano and Merano markets (to 6 Jan, per earlier notes) are the only full Alpine-style markets reachable for New Year.

### Cited Findings
- **Bologna, Antica Fiera di Santa Lucia** (Portico dei Servi, Strada Maggiore): 2025 edition ran 7 Nov–26 Dec 2025 (Bologna Welcome; another site says from 16 Nov). The 2026 edition is listed as **6 Nov–27 Dec 2026**, about 09:00–20:00, per a travel guide rather than an official notice — [Bologna Welcome markets blog](https://www.bolognawelcome.com/it/blog/i-mercatini-di-natale-in-centro-e-fuoriporta); [VoloScontato Bologna markets](https://www.voloscontato.it/sagre-ed-eventi/mercatini-di-natale-di-bologna-2025-quando-dove-e-info.php)

## 3. Holiday closures and bookings: Last Supper, Duomo, La Scala, Doge's Palace, St Mark's, Archiginnasio, Asinelli, Ferrari Museum

### Takeaway
25 Dec and 1 Jan are the closure days. 26 and 31 Dec are mostly normal (31 Dec may close early). The Last Supper is the hard one: closed Mon 28 Dec and 1 Jan, and the late-December release sold months ago. The only realistic routes are the weekly Wednesday 12:00 release or a guided-tour bundle. La Scala has *Otello* on 22 and 29 Dec and 2 and 5 Jan, but nothing found for 31 Dec. Bologna's Asinelli tower is closed, with no reopening date (2028 at best).

  - Adult ticket €15; €2 for ages 18–25; free for under-18s, with booking required.

  - A third-party guide says Nov–Jan tickets go on sale at the end of September, and that a limited batch of extra tickets is released **every Wednesday at 12:00 for the following week**.

## 4. New Year's Eve 2026: what each city does (last year's programmes) and ticketed gala dinners

### Takeaway
All four main cities have free outdoor NYE events (last year). They differ in style: a bonfire in Bologna, lagoon fireworks in Venice, a pop concert plus fireworks at the Arena in Verona, and only a capped "party" square in Milan with no concert. Ticketed options include La Fenice's concert (31 Dec, 16:00; 2025–26 prices €25–€500) and Verona's Teatro Filarmonico San Silvestro concert. Restaurant NYE menus in Italy ranged from €34 to €1,400 last year.

## 5. Venice specifics in late December: acqua alta, MOSE, fog, access fee

### Takeaway
There is a real but moderate chance of wet feet in St Mark's Square. It floods at about 80 cm, while MOSE is only raised for forecasts of about 110 cm and above, and winter 2025–26 saw frequent high tides. Major floods are prevented. No access fee applies on any date of this trip (the reported 2027 calendar starts 26 Mar).

### Cited Findings
- **MOSE:** raised for tides forecast above 110 cm (ArchDaily). About 12% of the city lies below 110 cm (Avvenire, 2018). Wikipedia cites 130 cm (earlier notes), a conflict; 110 cm is consistent with operational reporting — [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/994482/whatsapp:/send); [Avvenire](https://www.avvenire.it/attualita/venezia-rialza-piazza-san-marco-per-salvarla-dalle-maree_35196)
- **St Mark's Square floods at around 80 cm.** In February 2026, Lindipendente reported that MOSE costs about €200,000 per raising "but Piazza San Marco is always flooded", with 24 high-water events in 50 days — [L'Indipendente, 19 Feb 2026](https://www.lindipendente.online/2026/02/19/venezia-il-mose-costa-200mila-euro-ogni-alta-marea-ma-piazza-san-marco-e-sempre-allagata/?pdf=179546); [Sist'Art](https://sistart.org/news/st-marks-square-and-the-high-tide/)
- **An example sub-MOSE flood:** a 99 cm peak (forecast 105 cm) flooded the square during a storm (undated) — [Tio.ch](https://www.tio.ch/dal-mondo/cronaca/1780894/a-venezia-torna-l-acqua-alta-allagata-piazza-san-marco)
- **MOSE totals:** 156 closures by 15 May 2026, 32 of them in 2026 (earlier notes) — [MOSE, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOSE)
- **Access fee 2027:**
  - Reported as 72 days, Fri 26 Mar–Sun 15 Aug 2027 (La Voce di Venezia and Radio Clodia, 15 Oct 2026; Nordest24, 23 Oct 2026). Not yet adopted by resolution; maximum €10.
  - The 2026 scheme ran 3 Apr–26 Jul.
  - The official portal is cda.ve.it.
  — [Pixidia](https://pixidia.com/?p=92545); [Sky TG24, Apr 2026](https://tg24.sky.it/economia/2026/04/04/nuovo-contributo-accesso-venezia-come-funziona); [italia.it](https://italia.it/en/veneto/venice/things-to-do/information-contribution-access-venice)

### Inferences
- **Acqua alta:** high tides are usually morning or evening peaks of a few hours. Check the Comune's tide forecast (Centro Maree) daily, and pack or buy waterproof overboots. A flood could affect the St Mark's area on any day, but MOSE makes a city-wide flood very unlikely.
- **Access fee:** no fee and no QR code are needed on 26 Dec–2 Jan, whether staying overnight or day-tripping from Bologna.
- **Fog:** no sourced data; see Gaps. Late-December Po Valley and lagoon fog is common (general knowledge, unverified) and can delay vaporetti.

### Gaps
- No December 2025 acqua alta statistics were found. The "24 events in 50 days" figure is not dated precisely (Dec 2025–Feb 2026 is implied).
- No fog-day statistics were found.
- The official 2027 access-fee resolution had not been published.

## 6. Food highlights and halal options (Bologna, Milan, Venice)

### Takeaway
Bologna is the food winner for this week (tortellini in brodo is the Christmas and New Year dish; Modena and Parma are nearby). Its halal scene is "moderate", clustered in the university quarter, Bolognina and Piazza VIII Agosto. Milan has the most halal choice (Via Padova, Porta Venezia, Centrale). Venice's historic centre has only a few halal-declared places; Mestre has more, and the main mosque is in Marghera.

### Cited Findings
- **Bologna halal:**
  - HalalSpy rates the scene "moderate" and names Taj Mahal, La Baraka, Draben's Taco Halal and Pak Helal Kebab.
  - Main clusters are Via Mascarella and the university quarter, Bolognina (north of Bologna Centrale) and the Piazza VIII Agosto area, with halal butchers and groceries on Via Stalingrado and in Bolognina.
  - Most places self-declare halal without audit, so ask about the meat source.
  — [HalalSpy Bologna](https://halalspy.com/halal-food-guide/europe/bologna/); [Zabihah Taj Mahal Bologna](https://zabihah.com/restaurants/cb1868b3-7767-11ef-95ae-6045bdeb9f57/taj-mahal-bologna-emilia-romagna); [HalalSpy Italy](https://halalspy.com/halal-food-guide/europe/italy/)
- **Bologna mosque:** one guide names the Mosque of Bologna on Via Curtatone as the central community's anchor. A Bologna Centrale hotel lists halal food within 500 m — [HalalSpy Italy Muslim guide](https://halalspy.com/muslim-travel/europe/italy/); [HalalBooking SMY Bologna Centrale](https://halalbooking.com/en/smy-bologna-centrale/p/67568)
- **Bologna, other halal notes:** guides suggest local dishes such as tagliatelle and tortellini can be adapted in halal-friendly versions. A "halal tortellini" episode became a political controversy in Bologna. School canteens introduced halal meat in 2023 — [IAS Arabia PDF](https://iasarabia.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Halal-Food-Options-in-Bologna.pdf); [Libero](https://www.liberoquotidiano.it/news/terra-promessa/44015223/bologna_tortellini_islamici_halal_diventa_caso_politico/); [Dire](https://www.dire.it/20-09-2023/956063-a-bologna-arriva-la-carne-halal-nella-mensa-delle-scuole/)
- **Venice halal:**
  - In the historic centre, Orient Experience (Middle Eastern and Afghan, halal-declared) and ITAL INDIA Halal Foods (Indian and Pakistani).
  - Other guides name SKK Shock Kebab and Gerusalemme Pizza Kebab.
  - In Mestre, Fairouz (Lebanese) and halal butchers near the station; Mestre has more options than the island.
  - The main mosque is the Centro Islamico di Venezia in Marghera.
  - Some sources look generic, so verify before relying on them.
  — [HalalSpy Venice](https://halalspy.com/halal-food-guide/europe/venice/); [Bonjour Venise](https://bonjourvenise.fr/en/?p=2104)
- **Milan halal (earlier notes):** the most options of the three cities, along Via Padova, around Porta Venezia and near Milano Centrale — [HalalSpy Milan](https://halalspy.com/halal-food-guide/europe/milan/)
- **Food tours in Modena and Parma:**
  - The Parmigiano Reggiano Consortium site has a "book a visit" tool covering individual dairies, e.g., a guided tour and tasting at Caseificio Ugolotti in Parma.
  - Visits show the morning's cheese-making, the salting room and the ageing warehouse.
  - The big "Caseifici Aperti" open-dairies event is in spring (23–24 May 2026), not December.
  - No holiday-week opening data was found.
  — [Parmigiano Reggiano tour booking](https://www.parmigianoreggiano.com/dairies-book-a-guided-tour/product_id=296819); [Holidoit Parma dairy visit](https://www.holidoit.com/e/visita-al-caseificio-e-degustazione-di-parmigiano-reggiano-a-parma); [Reggio Emilia Welcome](https://www.reggioemiliawelcome.it/en/reggio-emilia/events/events-and-initiatives/folklore-and-festivals/open-dairies)

### Inferences
- **Halal-observant plan:** Bologna (moderate, walkable clusters near the station and university) and Milan (abundant) are easy. In Venice, stay near or eat in Mestre or Cannaregio, or use seafood and vegetarian Venetian dishes. Ask about wine in sauces and lard (strutto) in pastries.

A halal traveller would need a halal-adapted version, or tortelloni di ricotta (cheese-filled) as the typical meat-free Bolognese pasta. This is general culinary knowledge, not sourced this session.
- **Parmigiano dairy visits** usually happen in the morning when the cheese is made. Book via the Consortium tool for Tue 29 or Wed 30 Dec, and avoid 1 Jan (inference).

## 9. Short-range snow option over New Year (Cortina via Venice; Madonna di Campiglio / Val di Fassa via Verona–Trento)

Madonna di Campiglio is about 1h40 or more by bus from Trento, which is about an hour or more from Verona by train (unverified). Val di Fassa is reached by train to Ora, then bus.
