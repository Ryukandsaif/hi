# East & Southern Africa (Kenya, Tanzania/Zanzibar, Rwanda, Uganda, South Africa, Namibia, Botswana) as 1-week trips from the UAE, mid-Nov 2026 to early Feb 2027

Research note: today is 2026-11-08. About 27 web searches were run. **Page fetches were blocked** (gov.uk and other hosts fail DNS in this session), so every finding below comes from search-result summaries, not from reading full pages. Live FCDO/State Dept pages could not be opened directly; their content is reported as the search engine summarised it (sometimes via govdiff mirror pages). Older (pre-2025) or undated items are labelled. Anything marked "estimate" or "general knowledge" has no source in this research and should be treated as an assumption.

**Three big 2026-specific facts the report writer must not miss:**
1. **Ebola (Bundibugyo strain) is active in the region.** The DRC outbreak was detected in May 2026 and had passed 9,000 cases and 4,300 deaths by October. Uganda had its own outbreak, which WHO declared over on 27 Aug 2026. **Kenya confirmed 9 cases and 2 deaths (Nairobi, one family/contact cluster) between 6 and 23 Oct 2026.**
2. **A strong El Niño is under way.** ICPAC and the Kenya Met Department forecast above-normal Oct–Dec 2026 short rains over Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda and parts of Tanzania, possibly lasting until Feb 2027.
3. **New mandatory visitor health insurance (about USD 44) applies in Kenya (from about 5–6 Oct 2026, via the eTA) and Tanzania (from 1 Oct 2026).**

## 1. Wildlife and season fit by month (East Africa rains, Great Migration, gorillas, Botswana/Namibia green season, Kruger summer)

### Takeaway
For this window, the best wildlife match is **northern Tanzania (Ndutu/southern Serengeti calving) from about 20 Jan to early Feb 2027**. Late Nov–Dec 2026 in East Africa looks unusually wet because of a strong El Niño, which is bad for Mara/Serengeti road conditions and muddy gorilla treks. The migration also leaves the Mara by December, so the Mara is about resident game, not herds, in this window. Botswana's green season brings 40–60% lodge discounts (but not over Christmas/New Year) in exchange for heat (about 32°C) and afternoon storms.

### Cited Findings
**El Niño / short rains 2026 (major factor for late Nov–Dec)**
- ICPAC's 18 Aug 2026 outlook: increased likelihood of wetter-than-normal Oct–Dec (OND) 2026 conditions across the Greater Horn of Africa. Pacific SSTs indicate El Niño has developed and is expected to strengthen through the rest of 2026. The strongest wet signal is in the equatorial and southern zones (Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, parts of Tanzania). — [ICPAC](https://www.icpac.net/news/the-greater-horn-of-africa-is-expected-to-experience-a-wetter-than-normal-october-december-ond-2026-season-as-el-niño-strengthens/)
- Kenya Meteorological Department (26 Aug 2026 outlook): above-average rain expected over more than 80% of Kenya, with an 85–90% probability of above-normal rain in some areas. Short rains onset in most areas falls between October and November. KMD warned El Niño could extend to February 2027. — [Capital FM](https://capitalfm.africa/80pc-of-kenya-faces-above-average-rains-prolonged-wet-spells/); [K24](https://k24.digital/news/el-nino-kenya-met-warns-of-heavy-downpours-across-80-per-cent-of-the-country/amp); [Eastleigh Voice](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/headlines/383168/kenya-met-warns-of-increased-rains-from-october-as-strong-el-nino-event-looms)
- Kenya Met also told people to "prepare for heavy rainfall, floods and landslides in October" 2026. — [The Standard](https://thestandard.ke/sports/amp/environment-climate/2001557436/met-prepare-for-heavy-rainfall-floods-and-landslides-in-october)
- Forecast strength differs between sources: one report cites a 96% chance of a "very strong" El Niño during OND, while NOAA gives a 65% chance it becomes "strong or very strong" between Oct 2026 and Feb 2027. A positive Indian Ocean Dipole could amplify flooding. — [Citizen Digital explainer](https://citizen.digital/article/understanding-el-nino-the-ocean-pattern-reshaping-east-africas-rainy-season-n388679); [Welthungerhilfe July 2026 brief](https://www.welthungerhilfe.org/fileadmin/pictures/publications/en/fact-sheets/topics/2026-july-el-nino-potential-implications-briefing-whh.pdf)
- Tanzania Meteorological Authority (as summarised): northern and eastern areas get the OND short rains. For western, central and southern Tanzania (single rainy season), a strong El Niño is likely to bring the heaviest rains between November and at least February 2027. — [The Chanzo (search summary; attribution not fully verified)](https://thechanzo.com/?p=25764)
- Kenyan media framed the 2026 festive season as clouded by an "Ebola insurance row and El Niño". — [Nation](https://www.nation.africa/kenya/news/ebola-insurance-row-and-el-ni%C3%B1o-cloud-kenya-s-5-5m-tourist-ambition-as-festive-season-approaches-5635816)

**Great Migration location by month**
- December: the herds leave the Masai Mara and move into the southern Serengeti, then settle into the southern Serengeti/Ngorongoro Conservation Area to fatten before calving. — [Safari.co.za Dec map](https://www.safari.co.za/Great_Migration_Maps_By_Month-travel/great-migration-map-december.html); [Safari.co.za Jan map](https://www.safari.co.za/Great_Migration_Maps_By_Month-travel/great-migration-map-january.html)
- Calving: sources give "December to March, peaking mid-January to end of February" or "late January into March, most births in February". Ndutu sits on the boundary of Ngorongoro Conservation Area and Serengeti NP. Peak births are cited at about 8,000 calves a day. Timing depends on rainfall. — [Discover Africa calving FAQ](https://www.discoverafrica.com/blog/our-most-frequently-asked-questions-about-the-serengeti-calving-season/); [Wild Voyager](https://wildvoyager.com/journal/the-wildebeest-calving-explained/); [Horizon Guides Ndutu](https://horizonguides.com/tanzania/ndutu/experiences/calving-season-in-ndutu-plains); [Duma Explorer 2026 guide](https://www.dumaexplorer.com/blog/the-great-migration-in-east-africa)
- Masai Mara: herds typically arrive by early August, with river crossings about July–September. So Dec–Feb in the Mara means resident wildlife, not the migration. — [Duma Explorer](https://www.dumaexplorer.com/blog/the-great-migration-in-east-africa); [African Overland Tours month-by-month](https://africanoverlandtours.com/blog/month-by-month-guide-great-migration/)

**Gorillas (Rwanda/Uganda)**
- Rwanda's low-season gorilla discount period is officially "November to May" (RDB booking portal). Rwanda treats June–October as peak. — [RDB portal](https://visitrwandabookings.rdb.rw/rdbportal/low-season-discount); [KT Press, Jan 2026](https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/01/rwanda-floats-promotional-gorilla-trekking-rates-into-2026/)
- Uganda's discounted gorilla-permit months are reported as April, May and November. — [4x4 Uganda](https://www.4x4uganda.com/?p=4718); [Duma Explorer](https://www.dumaexplorer.com/blog/gorilla-trekking-permits-uganda-costs-booking-rules)
- The ICPAC wet-OND signal explicitly includes Rwanda and Uganda. — [ICPAC](https://www.icpac.net/news/the-greater-horn-of-africa-is-expected-to-experience-a-wetter-than-normal-october-december-ond-2026-season-as-el-niño-strengthens/)

**Botswana green season**
- Green season runs Dec–Mar per one operator, or late Oct–Mar/Apr per another. Lodges cut rates "by 40% to 60%" (another source: "sometimes by more than 50%"; a 2012 article said about 30%, now outdated). Single supplements are often waived. Value is described as excellent "excluding Christmas and New Year". — [Wanderlust](https://www.wanderlustmagazine.com/inspiration/green-season-in-botswanas-okavango-delta/); [Ultimate Travel Co](https://theultimatetravelcompany.co.uk/blog/why-green-season-travel-may-be-botswanas-hidden-gem); [Discover Africa](https://www.discoverafrica.com/blog/botswana-during-the-green-season); [Eclipse Travel](https://eclipsetravel.co.nz/the-magic-of-botswanas-green-season/)
- Weather: daytime about 32°C, nights about 19°C. Rain usually falls as short afternoon thunderstorms. The Delta is wetter, hotter, with heavy rains. One trip report found lodges less than half full. — [Wanderlust](https://www.wanderlustmagazine.com/inspiration/green-season-in-botswanas-okavango-delta/); [Luxury Travel Magazine](https://www.luxurytravelmagazine.com/news-articles/escape-winter-chill-with-green-season-botswana-safari)
- A green-season special example (Wetu "5 Rivers Special") ran 1 Dec 2025–15 Apr 2026, i.e. specials of this type are typical, but 2026–27 sheets were not found. — [search summary of Wetu pages](https://wetu.com/Itinerary/Overview/43d9861b-5a37-4f32-a46e-7fe7c95160b0)

### Inferences
- With a strong El Niño, late Nov–Dec 2026 in Kenya, northern Tanzania, Rwanda and Uganda carries real risk of heavy rain, black-cotton-soil mud, flooded crossings and lost game-drive time. Precedent from general knowledge (not verified here): the 2023 El Niño short rains flooded parts of the Mara and stranded vehicles. If KMD is right that El Niño runs into Feb 2027, even the usual Jan–Feb "short dry" spell may be less reliable than normal.
- Northern Tanzania (Ndutu) in late Jan–Feb is still the best wildlife bet: calving plus predator action. Ndutu is an open short-grass plain where game viewing does not depend on dry weather the way a river-crossing season does (general knowledge).
- Rwanda gorillas are bookable year-round, and Nov–May permits are discounted. Treks in Nov–Dec will be muddier. Jan–Feb is normally the "short dry season" (general knowledge, not verified here).
- Kruger summer (Nov–Feb): this research found no source. General knowledge (unverified): it is hot (often 30–40°C), it is the rainy season with thick, green bush that makes game harder to spot, it is peak malaria-risk season (Kruger/Lowveld is a malaria area), and it is the time of newborn impala and migrant birds. Private reserves (Sabi Sand) still give good sightings because off-road driving is allowed.
- Okavango flood timing: no source found. General knowledge (unverified): Angolan floodwaters reach the Delta about March–June and peak about June–August. Dec–Feb is low water, rain-fed and green, so mokoro (canoe) trips may be limited at some camps.
- Namibia Dec–Feb: no source found. General knowledge (unverified): very hot in the desert (Sossusvlei often above 35–40°C), summer thunderstorms in the central/northern interior, Etosha green with game dispersed. Long driving distances make one week tight.
- El Niño and southern Africa: general knowledge (unverified here) is that El Niño years tend to bring hotter, drier summers to southern Africa. That may mean fewer rain interruptions in Botswana/Kruger but more heat and possible drought stress.

### Gaps
- No 2026–27 source found for Kruger summer conditions, Namibia summer heat/rain or Okavango flood timing. Those points above are general knowledge only.
- Whether the forecast El Niño short rains have actually been heavy so far in Oct–early Nov 2026 (observed rainfall and flooding) was not checked.
- The WMO's September 2026 El Niño update was not checked, so forecast strength may have shifted.

## 2. South Africa summer: Cape Town, Winelands, Garden Route, Cape Point; load-shedding status; safety

### Takeaway
Dec–Feb is Cape Town's dry, sunny high season (highs about 25–35°C). The strong south-easter ("Cape Doctor") wind is its main weather downside. **Mid-Dec to mid-Jan is the most crowded and expensive period** (South African school holidays). Load-shedding has effectively ended: Eskom forecast a load-shedding-free 2025/26 summer, and a May 2026 headline reported a full year without it. UAE nationals are visa-exempt for up to 90 days. This research found no current 2026 crime-advisory text.

### Cited Findings
- December is one of Cape Town's driest months. The south-easter ("Cape Doctor") is a strong, dry wind, strongest in summer. It clears pollution but spreads brush fires in Table Mountain National Park. — [Come to Cape Town, December weather](https://www.cometocapetown.com/why-cape-town/visitor-info/weather-in-cape-town/december-weather); [Rough Guides](https://www.roughguides.com/articles/cape-town-weather-december-travel-tips/)
- Typical summer highs are about 25–35°C, with occasional spikes near 40°C (visitor-guide figures). — [Rough Guides](https://www.roughguides.com/articles/cape-town-weather-december-travel-tips/); [KimKim](https://kimkim.com/c/south-africa-in-december-travel-tips-weather-more)
- Mid-Dec to mid-Jan is the festive season, with domestic holidaymakers, peak crowds and the year's highest accommodation prices. January is also peak. Book restaurants and rooms well ahead. — [KimKim](https://kimkim.com/c/south-africa-in-december-travel-tips-weather-more); [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/africa/south-africa/articles/whens-the-best-time-of-year-to-visit-cape-town)
- Hotel guides advise booking at least 2–3 months ahead for peak Cape Town stays. "In December and January in particular, Cape Town is not wallet-friendly" (Moneyweb-sourced piece). — [Hotelier's Choice Christmas guide](https://hotelierschoice.com/guides/cape-town/christmas/); [Herald (Zim), via Moneyweb](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/south-africans-are-being-priced-out-of-holidaying-in-cape-town/)
- Load-shedding: Eskom's official 2025/26 summer outlook (1 Sep 2025–31 Mar 2026) forecast no load-shedding, after 13 days of load-shedding the previous summer. — [SAnews](https://www.sanews.gov.za/node/77000); [Engineering News, 5 Sep 2025](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/no-loadshedding-foreseen-this-summer-as-eskom-generation-recovery-plan-boosts-its-performance-2025-09-05)
- A May 2026 headline reported "South Africa marks a full year without load shedding". Separately, an Energybee analysis (26 Mar 2026) said Eskom warned of possible regional load-shedding if demand cannot be met; that is the only risk signal, and no figures were given. — [Energybee](https://energybee.co.za/news/eskom-summer-outlook-2026-analysis); [The Citizen, 2025 summary](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/eskom-powers-through-2025-with-minimal-load-shedding); ["full year" headline URL not confirmed; possibly TechCentral](https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-spared-summer-blackouts/269734/)
- Entry: UAE ordinary-passport holders have had visa-free entry to South Africa since August 2019 (UAE MOFA), for stays up to 90 days (Gulf News citing SA Home Affairs). A July 2026 guide confirms this is still the case for tourism/business up to 90 days. A UAE residence permit alone does not qualify. — [UAE MOFA](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Missions/Pretoria/UAE-Relationships); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/emiratis-exempt-from-pre-entry-visa-to-south-africa-1.65809726); [SafariFind 2026](https://safarifind.com/blog/do-emirati-citizens-need-a-south-africa-visa-in-2026)
- Passport validity rules conflict: 6 months from entry (one guide) vs 30 days beyond the return date (SA Embassy, as cited). — [SafariFind 2026](https://safarifind.com/blog/do-emirati-citizens-need-a-south-africa-visa-in-2026)

### Inferences
- **Best Cape Town windows:** about 20 Nov–12 Dec 2026 (summer weather, pre-festive rates, Winelands at their best) and about 15 Jan–early Feb 2027 (after SA schools return, warm sea, still dry). Christmas–New Year is doable but costs the most and needs bookings now (within the 2–3-month lead time).
- A 7-day South Africa trip fits Cape Town (City Bowl, Bo-Kaap, V&A, Table Mountain, Cape Point/Chapman's Peak, Boulders penguins) plus 2 nights in the Winelands (Franschhoek/Stellenbosch). Optionally replace the Winelands with 2–3 nights at a private Greater Kruger lodge (fly CPT–Skukuza/Hoedspruit/Nelspruit). The full Garden Route needs 3–4 extra days and does not fit well in one week alongside safari. (Itinerary logic, general knowledge.)
- For a malaria-free safari add-on in summer: Eastern Cape private reserves (e.g. Shamwari/Kariega area, near Port Elizabeth/Gqeberha) or the Waterberg (Welgevonden) are commonly used alternatives to Kruger. General knowledge, not verified here.
- Load-shedding now looks like a low-probability nuisance rather than a trip factor. Most upscale hotels have backup power anyway (general knowledge).

## 3. Zanzibar beach season and fitting safari + Zanzibar into 7 days

### Takeaway
Zanzibar's beach season matches the calving season well: **January–February is hot (about 24–33°C), sunny and dry**, while Nov–Dec is short-rains season (showers usually clear quickly). Late Dec–Feb is peak pricing. A 7-day Tanzania trip of about 4 nights safari (Arusha/Tarangire–Ngorongoro–Ndutu) plus 3 nights Zanzibar is realistic, using flydubai's direct flights to both Kilimanjaro (JRO) and Zanzibar (ZNZ) as an open-jaw.

### Cited Findings
- Jan–Feb is the "short dry season", statistically the warmest of the two dry periods, with rain least likely to disrupt beach time. The typical Dec–Feb range is about 24–33°C. Dec–Feb is peak season with crowded beaches and higher rates. November is a cheaper shoulder month. — [SafariBookings Zanzibar](https://www.safaribookings.com/zanzibar/best-time); [Duma Explorer Zanzibar weather 2026](https://www.dumaexplorer.com/blog/zanzibar-weather-seasons-winds-when-to-go); [The Africa Specialists](https://www.theafricaspecialists.com/zanzibar-and-islands/best-time-to-visit/); [The World Pursuit](https://theworldpursuit.com/best-time-to-visit-zanzibar/)
- December is contested: some guides place the dry season "late December through February", others put the short rains in Nov–Dec (brief, intense showers that clear into sunshine). Main long rains are Mar–May. — [Follow Alice](https://followalice.com/adventure-trips/zanzibar-beach-holidays/posts/when-is-the-best-time-to-visit-zanzibar); [The Africa Specialists](https://www.theafricaspecialists.com/zanzibar-and-islands/best-time-to-visit/)
- flydubai flies Dubai–Kilimanjaro (launch article: six weekly 737 MAX flights, Emirates codeshare, economy return from AED 1,395 ≈ USD 380; the year of that fare is not stated, so it may be stale). — [Tourism Update](https://www.tourismupdate.com/article/flydubai-adds-kilimanjaro-to-its-network)

Expedia shows Emirates DXB–ZNZ from USD 418 (date unclear). — [flydubai DXB–ZNZ](https://www.flydubai.com/en-ae/cheap-flights/flights-from-dubai-to-zanzibar/); [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/lp/flight-routes/emirates-from-dubai-intl-to-zanzibar-intl/ek/dxb/znz)

### Inferences
- **Model 7-day Tanzania itinerary, late Jan–early Feb 2027 (inference):**
  - Day 1: DXB–JRO on flydubai; night in Arusha or Tarangire.
  - Day 2: Tarangire.
  - Day 3: Ngorongoro Crater descent (USD 295 vehicle fee).
  - Days 4–5: Ndutu calving plains, 2 nights.
  - Day 5 or 6: light aircraft Ndutu/Manyara–Zanzibar.
  - Days 6–8: Stone Town plus a north/east-coast beach, 2–3 nights.
  - Return ZNZ–DXB on flydubai.
- A Kenya–Zanzibar combo is also possible (Nairobi–Zanzibar flights exist; general knowledge), but Tanzania-only keeps it to one visa/insurance regime and puts the migration on the itinerary.
- For Dec 2026, Zanzibar is fine from about 20 Dec onwards (dry season starts, but Christmas pricing). Late Nov–mid Dec risks showers, made more likely this year by El Niño.

## 4. Costs: lodges/camps, park fees, gorilla permits, Cape Town 5-star, and how far USD 4,000+ goes

### Takeaway
A "good" East African safari costs real money. **Premium Mara conservancy camps run about USD 900–1,500 per person per night, all-inclusive. Mid-range named camps start around USD 900–1,370, and ultra-luxury (Angama-class) is USD 1,200–2,500 or more.** On top of that come reserve/park fees: Mara USD 200/day through Dec 2026 and USD 100/day Jan–Jun; Serengeti about USD 70–83/day plus about USD 71/night concession; Ngorongoro crater USD 295 per vehicle. A USD 4,000-per-person budget with flights covers a mid-range private Tanzania safari plus Zanzibar, a Cape Town + Winelands week at 4–5-star level, or about 3 nights in a premium Mara conservancy. It does **not** cover a full week of luxury camps, or a Rwanda gorilla trip at luxury level (the USD 1,500 permit alone is a big slice).

### Cited Findings
**Kenya / Masai Mara**
- Masai Mara National Reserve, non-resident adult: **USD 100/day Jan–Jun 2026 and USD 200/day Jul–Dec 2026** (Narok County; sources broadly agree). Child rates vary by source (USD 50 to USD 100). Mara Triangle charges the same adult rates. Vehicle, camping and balloon-landing fees are extra. — [MasaiMara.travel 2026 & 2027 fees](https://www.masaimara.travel/park-entry-fees.php); [Kenya Times, Sep 2026](https://thekenyatimes.com/lifestyle/travel-tourism/masai-mara-entrance-fees-and-park-charges-2026-updated-for-tourists/); [East Africa Safaris & Tours](https://www.eastafricasafaristours.com/masai-mara-park-entrance-fees-2026)
- Mara conservancy fees (separate from the reserve, paid to landowners): typically USD 80–150 per person per night. One camp publishes USD 130 per adult. — [Resident, Feb 2026](https://resident.com/resource-guide/2026/02/07/ultra-luxe-conservation-safari-in-masai-mara-2026s-legacy-destination); [Kenya Times cost breakdown, Sep 2026](https://thekenyatimes.com/lifestyle/travel-tourism/masai-mara-safari-cost-2026-complete-budget-breakdown-for-kenya-trips/)

## 8. Country verdicts, month-by-month scores and best windows (synthesis)

### Takeaway
For a UAE traveller wanting culture, cities and food plus nature/adventure in one week:
- **South Africa (Cape Town + Winelands, optional private-reserve safari) scores highest overall (8.5/10)**, best 20 Nov–12 Dec 2026 or 15 Jan–early Feb 2027.
- **Tanzania (Ndutu calving + Ngorongoro + Zanzibar) is the top wildlife pick (8/10)**, best about 20 Jan–10 Feb 2027.

Frank verdicts:
- **South Africa: the best all-rounder.**
  - Strong culture and food: Bo-Kaap, V&A, Winelands, the halal scene.
  - Dramatic nature: Table Mountain, Cape Point, penguins, whale-free season but great beaches and hikes.
  - Easiest entry (visa-free) and a direct Emirates flight.
  - Weaknesses: festive peak prices, the south-easter wind, and summer Kruger being hot, thick-bushed and malarial.
- **Tanzania: the best "wow" safari.** Calving season is world-class, and Zanzibar adds Swahili-Arab culture, Stone Town and halal food. Weakness: recent political volatility (now calm per the FCDO), new insurance and screening formalities, and a fixed window that only opens from about mid/late January.
- **Kenya:** great guiding and conservancies, but in this window you pay premium prices for resident game in possibly very wet conditions, with a fresh Nairobi Ebola cluster. It is worth it only from mid-January.
- **Rwanda:** gorillas are a bucket-list experience, Kigali is clean and safe-feeling (general knowledge), and Nov–May discounted permits help. But a week there is mostly a single-experience trip, it is wet before January, and it sits next door to the region's largest Ebola outbreak and the DRC conflict.
- **Botswana:** superb wilderness and green-season discounts, but no city/culture component, heat and storms, and still expensive.
- **Namibia:** spectacular but a long-distance self-drive in high summer heat; needs about 10+ days.
- **Uganda:** cheaper gorilla permits, but a US Level 4 advisory, long road transfers to Bwindi and unclear 2027 permit pricing make it a poor 1-week pick now.
