## 1. Season fit by month (late Nov 2026, Dec 2026, Jan 2027, early Feb 2027)

### Takeaway
For warm weather the best options are Sri Lanka's south/west coast and Hill Country, the Maldives (dry season from about December) and Rajasthan/Goa/Kerala, all at their best from December to early February. Bhutan and Nepal are dry, clear and cold in this window. Mauritius and Seychelles are in their wettest, cyclone-prone (Mauritius) or northwest-monsoon (Seychelles) season. Gulmarg's skiing really starts late December and is best from mid-January to February.

### Cited Findings
**Sri Lanka**
- The northeast monsoon brings rain to the east coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay) from roughly October to January and peaks in November–December. The south/west coast (Colombo, Bentota, Hikkaduwa, Galle, Mirissa) and Yala are in their dry season, with January–March the driest months and December–March the best window for the west and south coasts. — [Wego monsoon guide 2026](https://blog.wego.com/sri-lanka-monsoon-season/); [Climates to Travel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/sri-lanka); [kimkim, Sri Lanka in December](https://kimkim.com/c/sri-lanka-in-december-travel-tips-weather-and-more)
- One source gives Trincomalee about a 48% chance of significant rain on any December day. Sources disagree on when the east-coast monsoon ends: one says late January or February. — [Champion Traveler, Trincomalee](https://championtraveler.com/dates/best-time-to-visit-trincomalee-lk/); [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/sri-lanka-monsoon-season/)
- Late-November risk is real. Cyclone Ditwah struck in late November 2025 and caused heavy damage to the upcountry railway (more than 20 landslides on the Badulla line). The northern railway line was suspended for 24 days and reopened on 22 Dec 2025. — [Fantasia Tours](https://fantasiatours.com/blogs/view-blog/where-resilience-rides-the-rails-the-living-story-of-sri-lanka-railways); [Daily Mirror](https://www.dailymirror.lk/news-features/How-Cyclone-Ditwah-stole-the-beauty-of-upcountry-railway-line/131-328878); [News1st, 22 Dec 2025](https://english.newsfirst.lk/2025/12/22/northern-railway-line-reopens-after-24-day-suspension-due-to-cyclone-ditwah)
- The Sunday Times (Sri Lanka) reported in mid-December 2025 that "visitors pour in despite some setbacks in scenic cities and train rides", so tourism carried on after the cyclone. — [Sunday Times LK, 14 Dec 2025](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/251214/news/visitors-pour-in-despite-some-setbacks-in-scenic-cities-and-train-rides-624833.html)

**Maldives**
- December–April is the dry season. One planning guide advises booking 4–6 months ahead for that period and up to a year ahead for Christmas and New Year. — [amaldives.com dry-season guide 2026](https://amaldives.com/blog/maldives-dry-season-guide-2026); [amaldives Christmas/NY 2026](https://amaldives.com/blog/maldives-christmas-new-year-2026)

**Mauritius**
- Sources disagree on the cyclone season. One gives 15 Nov–30 Apr (South-West Indian Ocean basin rules). A Mauritius real-estate site says the official Mauritius Meteorological Services period is 1 Nov–15 May. — [GetYourTours Mauritius](https://getyourtoursmauritius.pages.dev/blog/mauritius-cyclone-season); [real-estate-mauritius.mu](https://real-estate-mauritius.mu/en/cyclone-season-mauritius/)
- No official 2026–27 seasonal cyclone forecast had been published as of 2 Sept 2026, when that site last checked official sources. The latest Mauritian outlook covered only rainfall and temperature. — [real-estate-mauritius.mu](https://real-estate-mauritius.mu/en/cyclone-season-mauritius/)
- Travel blogs put the highest cyclone and rain risk in January–March, with December also active. One blog claims a direct hit roughly every 3–5 years, which is not an official statistic. A climate summary gives about 210 mm of rain in January. December and early January are described as very crowded and expensive. Official-sounding guidance warns that short heavy downpours can flood low-lying urban areas. — [weather2travel](https://www.weather2travel.com/holidayweather/mauritius/?Units=1); [Mauritius.com weather](https://mauritius.com/discover/the-weather-in-mauritius); [Expat.com rain safety](https://www.expat.com/en/expat-mag/11260-a-comprehensive-guide-on-how-to-keep-safe-during-heavy-and-torrential-rain-in-mauritius.html)

**Seychelles**
- The northwest monsoon runs roughly November–March, and December and January are the wettest months. Rain usually falls in short, sharp bursts and temperatures stay at 24–31°C all year. December to early January is the busiest and most expensive period. Several guides prefer April–May and October–November. One rates December "go for it" but January the hardest month. Another says December–February is good for fishing. — [Divergent Travelers](https://www.divergenttravelers.com/best-time-to-visit-seychelles/); [Audley](https://www.audleytravel.com/us/the-seychelles/best-time-to-visit); [koek.sc](https://koek.sc/seychelles-weather-guide/best-time-to-visit-seychelles); [World Travel Guide](https://www.worldtravelguide.net/guides/africa/seychelles/weather-climate-geography)

**Bhutan**
- Winter runs December–February and January is usually the coldest month. Paro has some of the clearest skies of the year, with virtually no rain in December and January. Days are sunny and nights fall just below freezing. Sources disagree on crowds: one says January–February bring Paro's highest visitor numbers, another says December is quiet. — [kimkim, Bhutan in December](https://kimkim.com/c/bhutan-in-december-travel-tips-weather-and-more); [Green Bhutan Tours, January](https://greenbhutantours.com/bhutan-weather/bhutan-weather-in-january); [Druk Asia, December](https://www.drukasia.com/bhutan/bhutan-weather-in-december/); [WhenVerdict, Bhutan January](https://whenverdict.com/visit/bhutan/in/january/)

**Nepal**
- Poon Hill (about 3,210 m) is widely called the best winter trek: cold but rarely dangerous. Everest Base Camp is possible in winter but nights fall to about −20°C at Base Camp (5,364 m) and about −24°C at Gorakshep, and snow can cover the trail for days. December is slightly milder than January. Crowds thin by December, and daylight is short, so treks start early. All of these are trekking-agency sources. — [kimkim winter treks](https://www.kimkim.com/c/the-best-winter-treks-in-nepal); [kimkim EBC in December](https://www.kimkim.com/c/everest-base-camp-in-december-travel-tips-weather-and-more); [Mosaic Adventure, Poon Hill Nov/Dec](https://mosaicadventure.com/poon-hill-trek-nepal/poon-hill-weather-november-and-december/); [Breeze Adventure EBC Dec–Feb](https://www.breezeadventure.com/blog/everest-base-camp-trek-in-december-january-and-february-weather-and-cost)

**India: Kashmir/Gulmarg snow**
- Gulmarg's ski season typically runs from late December to early March, with the best snow from mid-January to late February. Season dates and lift schedules depend on weather and operators. Winter flights into Srinagar can be disrupted (keep a spare day), and weather often closes the gondola and the Tangmarg road. — [HappyFares, Gulmarg skiing 2026–27](https://happyfares.in/blog/gulmarg-skiing-2026-27-srinagar-flights/); [Zoutons Gulmarg guide 2026](https://zoutons.com/news/gulmarg-trip-guide-2026)
- A report from mid-October 2026 said Gulmarg ski resort and the Zojila and Razdan passes were covered in fresh snow. I found no official 2026–27 opening notice. — [NewKerala, Nov 2026](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/first-snowfall-season-hits-jk-mountain-passes-gulmarg-961.htm)
- A January 2026 local report said about 3.5 ft of snow had built up in the Gulmarg bowl and officials expected to run the season. Another report said Gulmarg was "fully booked" with Sonamarg at 70% occupancy during a snow spell (undated in my results). — [Greater Kashmir, slopes reopening](https://m.greaterkashmir.com/article/gulmarg-readying-for-mega-events-as-ski-slopes-set-to-reopen-from-sunday/471859); [Greater Kashmir, fully booked](https://m.greaterkashmir.com/article/gulmarg-fully-booked-sonamarg-at-70-occupancy-as-tourists-flock-to-kashmir-snow/473516)
- Snow reliability is a growing concern. Deccan Herald ran a feature headlined "After the boom, the melt: how warmer winters are reshaping Kashmir's tourism economy" (title only seen). — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/jammu-and-kashmir/after-the-boom-the-melt-how-warmer-winters-are-reshaping-kashmirs-tourism-economy-3899365)

**India: Goa**
- Early December has near-zero rain, a calm sea and rates below peak. Peak runs 20 Dec–5 Jan at 27–32°C. This comes from one Indian travel-booking blog. — [HappyFares Goa December 2026](https://happyfares.in/blog/goa-december-travel-guide-india-2026/)

### Inferences
- Month-by-month (my synthesis):
  - **Late Nov 2026:** Rajasthan (Pushkar), Nepal (still the end of the clear post-monsoon trekking season), Goa and Bhutan are good. Sri Lanka's south and west are acceptable but carry tail-end storm risk, as Ditwah showed. The Maldives is transitional. Mauritius and Seychelles are humid and wet but before their January peak. Gulmarg has snow but no proper skiing yet.
  - **Dec 2026:** Bhutan (festivals, clear skies), Rajasthan, Kerala, Goa and the Sri Lankan south/west and Hill Country are strong. The Maldives is dry but expensive after about 20 Dec. Nepal's lower treks are fine. Seychelles and Mauritius are wet.
  - **Jan 2027:** Sri Lanka's south and west are at their driest, the Maldives is in dry season after the festive peak, Rajasthan has JLF, and Gulmarg has its best snow. Mauritius is at peak cyclone and rain risk.
  - **Early Feb 2027:** Sri Lanka, the Maldives and Gulmarg are all strong. Bhutan has its early-February festivals (Punakha/Trongsa). Rajasthan is pleasant. Mauritius and Seychelles are still wet.
- Kerala's main rains (southwest monsoon June–Sept; northeast monsoon Oct–Nov) are normally over by December, which is why Dec–Feb is considered peak season. This is my general knowledge and **was not confirmed by any source in this research**.

- No ENSO or seasonal outlook for 2026–27 was checked.

## 3. Costs: luxury and 4–5★ rates, fees, and how far USD 4,000+ per person goes

### Takeaway
Sri Lanka, India and Nepal let a USD 4,000 per-person budget buy genuine luxury for a week, including flights. Bhutan is fine at a mid-to-upper level (the SDF alone is USD 100 per night) but not at Aman or Six Senses level. The Maldives works on that budget only in January or early February at upper-mid resorts with a shared villa. Over Christmas and New Year, prices are roughly 1.5–2x, and with festive minimum stays and gala dinners a good resort clearly exceeds USD 4,000 per person.

### Cited Findings
**Bhutan**
- The SDF is **USD 100 per person per night** in 2026. Several sources say the reduced rate runs to **31 Aug 2027**; one gives December 2027. It was halved from USD 200 in 2023. Children 6–11 pay half and under-6s are exempt. Indian citizens pay Nu 1,200 per night. One outlier page still lists USD 200 and appears outdated. — [Little Bhutan SDF guide 2026](https://littlebhutan.com/travel-guide/bhutan-sdf/); [The Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/40070380); [Windhorse Tours FAQ](https://www.windhorsetours.com/faq/what-is-the-sdf-sustainable-development-fees-and-how-much-is-it/); outlier [TravelArbitrage](https://www.travelarbitrage.net/en/blog/bhutan-visa-permit-system-2026)
- 2026 cost guides report a 5% GST on tour-package components since January 2026. The SDF itself is exempt. — [Little Bhutan trip cost 2026](https://littlebhutan.com/bhutan-trip-cost/); [Little Bhutan SDF](https://littlebhutan.com/travel-guide/bhutan-sdf/)
- &Beyond Punakha River Lodge advertises an SDF credit for stays of 3 nights or more (title seen; current validity not checked). — [&Beyond offer](https://www.andbeyond.com/offers/stay-3-sdf-free-at-punakha/)

**Maldives**
- **Green tax** since 1 Jan 2025: USD 12 per adult per night at resorts (up from 6) and USD 6 at guesthouses and hotels (up from 3). Under-2s are exempt. I found no further green-tax rise or new departure tax in 2026. A May 2026 trade page mentions TGST at 17% and the green-tax doubling, but appears to describe the 2025 changes. — [PSM News](https://cms.psmnews.mv/en/172263); [MMTV](https://en.mmtv.mv/4549); [Travel Trade Maldives](https://www.traveltrademaldives.com/?p=62863)
- Green-tax revenue was USD 84.31m in the first seven months of 2026, up 5.8%, which suggests strong arrivals. — [Maldives Voice](https://maldivesvoice.mv/3149); [Miadhu](https://miadhu.mv/en/articles/14008)
- **Festive premium:** festive weeks run 50–100% above standard peak; a villa at USD 800 in January can reach USD 1,500 over the holidays. One planning guide gives "premium mid-range" resorts USD 1,800–4,000+ per night for 20 Dec–10 Jan versus USD 1,500–3,500+ for 11 Jan–28 Feb. A lower-quality site says festive rates are about 2x normal, e.g., Soneva Fushi at USD 1,200–2,000 festive versus 600–1,000 normal. All of these are blog or affiliate estimates. — [Hotels for Kings](https://hotelsforkings.com/best/maldives-hotel-prices-when-to-book/); [amaldives Christmas/NY 2026](https://amaldives.com/blog/maldives-christmas-new-year-2026); [immaldives (low quality)](https://immaldives.pages.dev/experiences/ultra-luxury/festive-season)
- **January 2026 examples** (amaldives estimates): one resort's water villa at USD 2,500–5,000+ per night B&B, another's beach villa at USD 1,200–1,800 per night all-inclusive. Leaving a few days after New Year avoids the rush. — [amaldives best resorts January 2026](https://amaldives.com/blog/best-maldives-resorts-january-2026)
- **Festive add-ons, 2025–26 example (Holiday Inn Kandooma):** minimum 4-night stay, compulsory Christmas Eve gala at USD 250++ per adult and New Year's Eve gala at USD 350++ per adult. Other guides cite USD 300–800 per person for Christmas Eve and USD 500–1,500+ for NYE. — [Holiday Inn Resort Kandooma](https://maldives.holidayinnresorts.com/?p=3554)
- **Transfers:**
  - Seaplane: about USD 400–700 per person return; USD 600–850 for remote resorts 150 km or more out.
  - Speedboat (near Malé): USD 150–400, with some as low as USD 50–100.
  - Domestic flight: USD 110–540 plus a speedboat.

## 6. Practicalities: internal transfers and time sinks

### Takeaway
The biggest time sinks are:
- **Maldives:** seaplane or domestic-flight transfers that add cost and (my general knowledge) only run in daylight.
- **Bhutan:** Paro is the only international gateway and depends on visibility.
- **Sri Lanka:** road travel replaces the broken Kandy–Nanu Oya rail section.
- **Kashmir:** weather can close Srinagar flights, the Gulmarg gondola and the road.

### Cited Findings
- **Bhutan:** Paro flights fly under visual flight conditions. Poor visibility, cloud and weather can disrupt operations, especially in monsoon and winter, so leave slack. One itinerary guide says winter flights run more smoothly early in the day. — [kimkim Bhutan December](https://kimkim.com/c/bhutan-in-december-travel-tips-weather-and-more); [Druk Asia](https://www.drukasia.com/bhutan/bhutan-weather-in-december/)
- **Maldives:** many resorts include transfers in room rates and others bill separately. A domestic flight plus speedboat is the alternative for distant atolls. Seaplane baggage is capped at about 20–25 kg. — [Let's Go Maldives](https://letsgomaldives.com/blogs/maldives-airport-transfer-costs/); [Maldives Magazine, Lhaviyani](https://maldives-magazine.com/general-info/seaplane-vs-speedboat-lhaviyani-cost-time-luggage.htm)
- **Sri Lanka rail:** no direct Kandy–Ella train as of October 2026. Use car or bus to Nanu Oya, then train to Ella. — [Big World Small Pockets](https://www.bigworldsmallpockets.com/?p=55047)
- **Kashmir:** keep a spare day for Srinagar flight disruption. The gondola and Tangmarg road often close for weather. — [HappyFares Gulmarg](https://happyfares.in/blog/gulmarg-skiing-2026-27-srinagar-flights/)
- **Nepal:** short December days require early starts on treks. — [kimkim winter treks](https://www.kimkim.com/c/the-best-winter-treks-in-nepal)

### Inferences
- **A 1-week Sri Lanka loop** fits without exhaustion: Colombo/Galle, south coast, Yala, Ella and Nuwara Eliya, and Kandy or Sigiriya (Sigiriya being dry-zone and fine in Jan–Feb). Expect 3–5 hours on roads between hubs. This is my estimate; road times were not sourced.
- **Maldives with a 7-night stay:** a late-evening arrival at Malé can force an airport-hotel night if the resort needs a seaplane. Aim for arrivals before about 15:00.
- **Bhutan in 6–7 nights:** Paro, Thimphu and Punakha are realistic. Adding Trongsa (for Trongsa Tshechu 17–21 Dec) means a long drive east (roughly 5–6+ hours from Punakha, my estimate) and fits only if the trip is built around it.
- **Rajasthan in 7 nights:** two or three cities at most (e.g., Jaipur–Jodhpur–Udaipur, or Jaipur–Ranthambore–Udaipur), with domestic flights via Delhi or Mumbai, or direct UAE–Jaipur flights if they exist (not verified).

- **Gulmarg (5.0):** the only real snow and ski option in the region, in a Muslim-majority valley, and best mid-January to February. Both UK and US advisories say do not travel, so insurance may be void. It is only for risk-tolerant travelers after checking UAE MOFA guidance.
- **Mauritius (5.5) and Seychelles (5.0):** beautiful, but this is their wet and cyclone season and prices peak at Christmas. Choose them only for late November to mid-December, and they are better saved for April–May or October–November.

### Gaps
- Scores are my own judgments and have not been validated against hotel and flight quotes for specific dates.
- The Mauritius and Seychelles verdicts lack price data.
- The Kerala score rests partly on unsourced general climate knowledge.
