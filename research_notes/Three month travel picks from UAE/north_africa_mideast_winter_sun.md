# North Africa, Nearby Middle East and Europe's Winter-Sun Spots: 1-Week Trips from the UAE, mid-Nov 2026 to early Feb 2027

Research date: 2026-11-08. Method: 25 web searches. Page fetches to gulfnews.com and khaleejtimes.com failed because those hosts are blocked in this session's network settings, so I did not fetch other pages. Every finding below comes from search-result summaries and snippets, not full-page reads. Treat exact figures as "reported by", and check them on the official site before booking. Anything labelled **[estimate]** or **[background, unverified this session]** comes from general knowledge, not from a source found in this session.

**Context that frames the whole note.** A US/Israel–Iran war began on 28 Feb 2026, when Dubai airport was briefly shut. A ceasefire in April 2026 was described as fragile. Gulf media were still running "US-Iran war: what UAE residents need to know" bulletins on 1 Oct 2026. Since 13 July 2026, Yemen's Houthis have run a missile and drone campaign against Saudi Arabia, which closed Riyadh airport from 10 to 25 Oct 2026. This changes the ranking: destinations reached by flying west, away from the Gulf (Morocco, Spain, Egypt), now look relatively better, and Saudi Arabia looks markedly worse for this season.

## Morocco: Marrakech, Fes, Chefchaouen, Sahara (Merzouga), High Atlas (Imlil, Oukaïmeden); weather, earthquake recovery, events, luxury hotels

### Takeaway
Morocco is the strongest all-round match for "culture, cities, food plus nature and adventure" this winter. It is booming (a record 19.8m tourists in 2025, still growing in 2026), it is far from the Gulf conflict, and it gains a headline event in late November: the 23rd Marrakech International Film Festival, 20–28 Nov 2026. The High Atlas luxury lodges have reopened after the 2023 quake. Village-level rebuilding is slower and less well documented. The main drawbacks are cold desert and mountain nights and long travel times (about 8–9 h from DXB **[estimate]**).

No Dec 2026 rate found.

### Inferences
- **Weather [background, unverified this session; typical climatology]:**
  - Marrakech in Dec–Jan: sunny days around 18–20°C, nights 5–7°C.
  - Fes and Chefchaouen: cooler (highs around 15–17°C) and the wettest part of the year (Nov–Feb), so Chefchaouen's blue lanes can be rainy.
  - Merzouga (Erg Chebbi): pleasant days around 18–20°C, but desert nights can fall to about 0–3°C. Luxury camps with heated tents and duvets matter.
  - High Atlas: snow is likely above about 2,000 m from Dec. Oukaïmeden skiing is snow-dependent and most reliable in Jan–Feb. Imlil trekking still works, but Toubkal summit attempts in winter need crampons and a guide.
- **Month by month:**
  - **Late Nov:** the best window. FIFM runs 20–28 Nov, the weather is still mild, and prices are pre-holiday.
  - **Dec:** good until about 18 Dec, then Christmas/New Year peak pricing and crowds in Marrakech.
  - **Jan:** coldest but cheapest after 6 Jan, and the best chance of Atlas snow.
  - **Early Feb:** the second-best window (milder, almond blossom starting, more Emirates capacity from 1 Feb). Ramadan 1448 is expected to start around 8 Feb 2027 **[estimate, see Muslim-friendly section]**, so trips should end before then unless the traveler wants a Ramadan experience.
- **Sample 7-night route:** Marrakech (3 nights), then Atlas/Ouarzazate/Dades, then Merzouga desert camp (2 nights), then out via Fes (2 nights). It needs a long drive or a domestic flight. A shorter alternative is Marrakech plus Atlas plus Agafay desert.
- **Budget [estimate]:** US$4,000+ per person comfortably covers DXB–CMN/RAK economy return (around US$600–1,000), 4–5 star riads (around US$200–500/night), a private driver and guide, and a luxury desert camp. It would even allow 1–2 nights at La Mamounia in early December. Royal Mansour for a full week would exceed the budget.
- **Verdict and score: 9/10.** Best window: **20–28 Nov 2026 (FIFM)** or **mid-Jan to ~5 Feb 2027**.

## Egypt: Cairo and the Grand Egyptian Museum (status and 2026 prices), Luxor/Aswan Nile cruises, Red Sea diving, price increases, safety

### Takeaway
Egypt has held up through the regional war. Q1 2026 arrivals rose 16% while the region fell 14%. December to February is peak Nile season. The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) is fully open, but its foreign/Arab adult ticket rose again on **1 Nov 2026**, to EGP 1,830 (about US$35–38), and entry needs a pre-booked timed slot. Luxor site prices are rising and sources disagree on them. The UK advises against travel only to North Sinai; the southern Red Sea resorts are rated low risk. Egypt is about a 3.5–4 h flight from the UAE **[estimate]**.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Weather [background, unverified]:**
  - Cairo in Dec–Jan: highs around 19–21°C, cool nights around 10°C.
  - Luxor and Aswan: highs around 23–26°C, which is ideal.
  - Red Sea (Hurghada, Sharm, Marsa Alam): highs around 22–24°C, water around 22–24°C. Diving is good, but a 5–7 mm wetsuit is advisable.
  - Egypt in Dec–Feb is the warmest option in this note for "warm" weather plus world-class culture.
- **Month by month:**
  - **Late Nov:** excellent, before peak prices. The GEM price rise has already happened.
  - **Dec:** peak, especially 20 Dec to 5 Jan. Nile cruise surcharges apply and Luxor is crowded.
  - **Jan:** peak continues but is still very good. Mid-to-late January is quieter than the holidays.
  - **Early Feb:** very good. Abu Simbel's sun festival is on 22 Feb, outside the window **[background]**.
- **Sample 7-night plan:** Cairo/Giza (2 nights, GEM plus pyramids), fly to Luxor, then a 4-night Luxor–Aswan cruise (or 3 nights Aswan–Luxor), with an optional Abu Simbel day. An alternative is 3 nights Cairo plus 4 nights Red Sea (Sharm or Marsa Alam) for diving.
- **Budget [estimate]:** US$4,000 per person covers economy flights from the UAE (around US$300–600 return), 2 nights at a 5-star Giza/Cairo hotel, a premium 5-star cruise like Soleil, domestic flights and guides. True ultra-luxury (Oberoi Philae, Sanctuary Sun Boat at Christmas) can push one person to US$4,000+ for the cruise alone.
- **Verdict and score: 8.5/10.** Best window: **late Nov–mid Dec 2026** or **mid-Jan–early Feb 2027**. Egypt is the most "big trip"-worthy destination within short-haul range of the UAE.

## Jordan: Petra, Wadi Rum, Dead Sea, Aqaba in winter; conflict impact 2025–2026; advisories; Jordan Pass

### Takeaway
Jordan was hit hard by the war. Petra bookings were 100% cancelled in March 2026 and about 80% down in April. The UK lifted its "against non-essential travel" advice in early June 2026, but the US remains at Level 3 "Reconsider Travel". Visitor numbers have partly recovered, but foreign arrivals were still down about 18% year on year through September (secondary source). For a UAE traveler that means quieter sites and likely deals. Petra and Wadi Rum are cold in Dec–Jan, so late Nov or early Feb is better. Jordan fits easily in 5–7 days and is about 3 h from the UAE **[estimate]**.

### Cited Findings
- **UK FCDO:** lifted its advice against non-essential travel to Jordan in early June 2026, and the Jordanian tourism ministry welcomed the change on 6 June — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/2646187/middle-east); [Petra News Agency](https://petra.gov.jo/wk/index.php/en/news/tourism-ministry-welcomes-uk-travel-advice-update-for-jordan). Before the war, the FCDO's only warning was for within 3 km of the Syrian border. Amman, Petra and Wadi Rum had no warnings — [same sources].
- **US State Department:** Level 3 "Reconsider Travel". Non-emergency US government staff and families were ordered to leave Jordan in early March 2026. The page was last updated 3 May 2026 — [government-data mirror](https://open-gov.usebase.io/ports/foreign/51105-al-aqabah-aqaba). Secondary sites say Level 4 applies only near the Syria and Iraq borders; this is not confirmed on travel.state.gov.
- **Impact on Petra:**
  - 100% of Petra bookings were cancelled in March 2026 — [Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/news/local/100-of-bookings-cancelled-in-petra-in-march-due-to-regional-tensions).
  - About 50% of May reservations were cancelled, but no hotels closed — [Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-109/News/Al-Sawa-er-50-of-May-Reservations-in-Petra-Canceled-No-Hotel-Closures-50468).
  - Q1 2026 Petra visitors were reported at about 180,000 (−23%). The source attribution is unclear in the search summary — [Akoni TV](https://akonitv.com/jordan-tourism-crisis-petra-decline/).
  - Jan–Sep 2026 Petra visitors were reported at 362,215 (−2.8%), with foreign visitors −17.8% and September at 43,901 (−8%). This is a secondary blog, so treat it with caution — [Nomad Lawyer](https://www.nomadlawyer.org/jordan-travel-industry-us-tourism-campaign-petra-recovery-2026).
  - For comparison, 2025 was a strong year: Petra visitors +27% and foreign arrivals +45% — [Petra News Agency](https://www.petra.gov.jo/en/news/petra-visitors-rise-27-in-2025-foreign-arrivals-up-45-pdtra).
- **Revenue rebound:** Jordan's tourism income rose 24.9% in July 2026 to US$926.2m, narrowing the year-to-date decline to −0.2% (preliminary Central Bank data) — [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2655083/business-economy).
- **Petra entry (official Jordan Tourism Board):** international visitors staying overnight pay **JD 50 for 1 day, JD 55 for 2 days, JD 60 for 3 days**. **Arab nationals pay a discounted JD 30** (relevant to a UAE passport). Under-12s enter free — [Visit Jordan blog](https://en.visitjordan.com/blog/external/7626/how-much-does-it-cost-to-go-to-petra-jordan).
- **Jordan Pass (third-party figures, not confirmed on jordanpass.jo):** Wanderer about JD 70, Explorer about JD 75, Expert about JD 80. The pass waives the visa fee for stays of 3+ nights and excludes Petra by Night — [eTravelSim 2026 budget guide](https://etravelsim.com/fr-fr/blogs/updates/jordan-travel-budget-7-day-trip-cost-in-2026).

### Inferences
- **For a UAE (Arab) passport holder,** the Jordan Pass is probably poor value. Arabs pay JD 30 at Petra, and GCC nationals likely do not pay the tourist visa fee **[background, unverified]**. Buy Petra tickets directly.
- **Weather [background, unverified]:**
  - Petra and Wadi Rum in Dec–Jan: highs around 10–14°C, nights near or below 0°C, with occasional snow at Petra. Rain can close the Siq for flash-flood risk.
  - Dead Sea: around 20–23°C.
  - Aqaba: around 21–23°C, and Red Sea diving and snorkelling are good.
  - Late Nov and early Feb are milder at altitude than late Dec–Jan.
- **Month by month:**
  - **Late Nov:** best (about 18–20°C days at Petra, quiet sites).
  - **Dec:** cold at Petra and Wadi Rum but crowd-free. Christmas is busier in Amman and Madaba.
  - **Jan:** coldest; the best Wadi Rum stargazing, but bring serious layers.
  - **Early Feb:** improving.
- **Sample 5–7 night route:** Amman/Jerash (1 night), Dana or the King's Highway, Petra (2 nights), Wadi Rum bubble camp (1 night), Aqaba or the Dead Sea (1–2 nights). Jordan is compact, so 7 nights is generous. It could also be a long weekend (Petra plus Wadi Rum in 4 days), which weakens its case as "the one big trip".
- **Safety logic:** Jordan sits between Israel, Syria, Iraq and Saudi Arabia. Iranian missiles and drones crossed Jordanian airspace in 2024–2026 conflicts **[background]**, and the US Level 3 reflects this. Risk to tourists at sites has been low historically, but flight disruption risk is real.
- **Verdict and score: 7.5/10** (late Nov: 8/10; Jan: 6.5/10). Best window: **15–30 Nov 2026** or **early Feb 2027**. It is a great 5-day trip and less compelling for a full 9-day "big trip" unless combined with Egypt (Aqaba–Nuweiba ferry or a short flight).

### Gaps
- The official 2026 Jordan Pass prices were not retrieved, and neither was the GCC/UAE visa treatment in Jordan.
- No winter 2026–27 booking data or official Petra visitor data for Oct 2026 was found.
- I could not confirm whether Jordanian airspace closures recurred after spring 2026.

## Oman (Muscat, Jebel Akhdar, Wahiba Sands, Musandam) and Saudi Arabia (AlUla, Hegra, Winter at Tantora/AlUla Moments 2026–27, Red Sea Global): worth a full week?

### Takeaway
**Oman:** Muscat and the interior are calm and still not covered by UK advice against travel. But Iran struck Oman twice in 2026 (Duqm on 12 July, and drones toward Musandam), and the US restricted its staff from Musandam and Duqm. Musandam, the area most reachable by road from the UAE, carries a real caveat. For a UAE resident, Oman is a superb long weekend or road trip but a weak use of "the one big trip".
**Saudi Arabia:** the season looks poor. In mid-Oct 2026 the UK began advising **against all but essential travel to Saudi Arabia**, after Houthi strikes since 13 July hit airports including Riyadh (closed 10–25 Oct). AlUla is not specifically named, and the 2026–27 AlUla Moments and Winter at Tantora dates are not yet published or were not found.

### Cited Findings
- **Oman, attacks:** Iran struck Oman twice in 2026, most recently on **12 July** (US logistics and refuelling facilities at Duqm; drones toward Musandam) — [Wego, 2026 update](https://blog.wego.com/is-it-safe-to-travel-to-oman/); [Iran International, 12 Jul 2026](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202607125582).
- **Oman, US Embassy:** issued a shelter-in-place advisory for Duqm and Musandam and restricted US government staff from travelling there, citing proximity to Iran and the risk of isolation — [UNI India](https://test.uniindia.com/us-embassy-in-oman-issues-shelter-in-place-advisory-for-duqm-and-musandam/world/news/3907357.html).
