## 1. Nationality-by-destination matrix and trip-blocking warnings (synthesis)

### Takeaway
For a one-week trip in Dec 2026 or Jan 2027, the easy destinations for all six GCC passports are Georgia, Türkiye, Armenia, Uzbekistan and (mostly) Azerbaijan, plus the UK with a £20 ETA. Three things need real attention:
- **Schengen:** a visa is still required for every GCC passport except the UAE. December appointments are the bottleneck.
- **China:** visa-free entry for Saudi, Omani, Kuwaiti and Bahraini nationals currently ends at 24:00 on 31 Dec 2026. As of late October 2026, no 2027 extension had been announced.
- **South Korea:** the temporary K-ETA exemption also ends 31 Dec 2026, and it is disputed whether GCC passports were ever covered by it.

**Realistic lead times for a trip booked in early November 2026:**
- **UK ETA:** apply at least 1 week before. Decisions are usually fast, but allow up to 3 working days.
- **Japan eVISA (Saudi):** about 5 business days.
- **Japan embassy visas (Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman):** not found. Plan for 1–2 weeks plus the embassy appointment.
- **Qatar pre-registration for Japan:** do it now.
- **Schengen:** appointment wait (2–6+ weeks in peak season) plus 15 days processing (up to 45).
  - A mid- or late-December trip is tight but possible if a slot is available within about 2 weeks.
  - A January trip is realistic if the applicant books an appointment immediately.
  - Saudi, Omani, Bahraini, Qatari and Kuwaiti travellers who already hold a valid multi-year Schengen visa avoid this entirely.
- **China (KSA/OM/KW/BH):** for January 2027, either wait for an extension announcement (likely late Nov or Dec; the 2025 extension notice was dated about 1 Dec 2025, per its URL) or apply for an L visa as a fallback.
- **K-ETA:** apply at least 72 hours before boarding [BK]. Safest to apply regardless of the exemption question.
- **Azerbaijan e-visa:** a few working days [BK]. Safest for UAE and Qatari nationals, given the conflicting sources.

## 2. Schengen: UAE visa-free status, ETIAS, EES, Qatar/Kuwait liberalisation, cascade rules, and appointment waits

### Takeaway
UAE nationals remain visa-free (no ETIAS yet), and everyone faces EES biometric registration, which has been live since 10 April 2026. Saudi, Omani, Bahraini, Qatari and Kuwaiti nationals still need Schengen visas. The Council only launched a conditional waiver process on 19 Oct 2026, with no date. December appointments from Gulf hubs are the main risk.

### Cited Findings
**ETIAS**
- The Commission's ETIAS page now says ETIAS is not operating and that its start date will be announced several months before launch. The earlier "last quarter of 2026" line has been removed. As of 19 October 2026, no date had been announced — [PAX News](https://www.paxnews.com/news/airline/etias-no-longer-has-confirmed-launch-date); [SchengenVisaInfo](https://schengenvisainfo.com/news/etias-has-no-launch-date-after-years-of-delays/)
- The Financial Times reported that ETIAS is likely delayed until 2027 and that eu-LISA considered a 2026 launch no longer feasible (FT article as relayed; not itself retrieved) — [PAX News](https://www.paxnews.com/news/airline/etias-no-longer-has-confirmed-launch-date); [SchengenVisaInfo](https://schengenvisainfo.com/news/etias-has-no-launch-date-after-years-of-delays/)
- ABTA, 13 Oct 2026: "there is now no official launch date for ETIAS". Industry insiders see April 2027 as the earliest realistic launch, and enforcement would follow about 6 months after launch with transitional flexibility. The enforcement point is a projection, not official — [ABTA](https://www.abta.com/tips-and-advice/planning-and-booking-a-holiday/upcoming-changes-travel-europe); [onevisacenter.com](https://onevisacenter.com/etias-requirements-us-citizens/); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexledsom/2026/07/30/traveling-to-europe-etias-is-delayed-as-ees-queues-bite/)

These conflict with the official position above — [etiasanswers.com](https://etiasanswers.com/etias-launch-date/); [travelmarketnews.com](https://travelmarketnews.com/etias-launch-timeline-update-2026); [europe-visa.eu](https://europe-visa.eu/blog/etias-launch-q4-2026-official-timeline-guide/); [globalworkpath.com (€20 fee)](https://globalworkpath.com/etias-requirements-2026/)

**EES**
- The Entry/Exit System has been fully operational at Schengen external borders since 10 April 2026, after a progressive rollout from 12 October 2025. Travellers should expect facial image and fingerprint capture and questions about their stay — [France Diplomatie](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/en/services-to-foreigners/visiting-france/ees-the-new-european-border-entryexit-system-goes-live-on-10-april-2026); [Georgetown OGS](https://globalservices.georgetown.edu/gps/travelresources/outboundvisas/update-eu-ees-etias/); [SchengenVisaInfo](https://schengenvisainfo.com/news/etias-has-no-launch-date-after-years-of-delays/)

**Qatar and Kuwait visa liberalisation**
- On 19 October 2026 (4203rd meeting), the Council of the EU approved conclusions launching a visa-waiver process for Qatar (doc ST 14629/26). There are parallel conclusions for Kuwait (ST 14626/26), and for Saudi Arabia, Bahrain and Oman. The Council will consider lifting the visa obligation only "upon the complete fulfilment of the benchmarks". No date has been set — [Council doc ST 14629/26](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-14629-2026-INIT/en/pdf); [VisasNews](https://visasnews.com/en/schengen-visas-eu-opens-door-to-visa-free-travel-for-five-gulf-countries/); [Agence Europe](https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13965/32/eu-council-approves-launch-of-visa-exemption-procedure-for-five-gulf-countries)

### Inferences
- No GCC passport needs ETIAS for Dec 2026 or Jan 2027 travel. Even if ETIAS were announced tomorrow, it would launch "several months" later with a transition period.
- Applying in early November 2026:
  - **Late-December trip:** feasible only if an appointment is available within about 2 weeks, given 15 days of processing. Applicants should check slots daily and consider premium or "prime time" slots, which VFS sells at extra cost [BK].
  - **January trip:** realistic if booked now.
  - The application normally has to go to the consulate of the main destination (most nights), so "shopping" for a faster consulate is limited [BK].
- GCC nationals holding a valid multi-year Schengen visa (from the cascade) can travel without a new application. This is the most important fact to check first.
- Allow extra time at the first Schengen entry point for EES registration, especially at peak holiday dates.

### Gaps
- No official Dec 2026 appointment wait times were found for VFS/TLS in Dubai, Riyadh, Doha or Kuwait City. Live VFS/TLS booking pages could not be fetched.

- Whether EES applies identically in Norway, Iceland and Switzerland was not re-verified. These are Schengen members, so it should [BK].

## 3. United Kingdom: ETA for GCC nationals

### Takeaway
All six GCC passports need a UK ETA, now mandatory and fully enforced. It costs £20 (since 8 April 2026), is valid for 2 years or until passport expiry, and is usually decided quickly (allow up to 3 working days). Jordan was moved from ETA to a full visa requirement in 2024. Egypt needs a visa.

### Cited Findings
- The ETA replaced the Electronic Visa Waiver for nationals of Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the UAE. Qatar was first (applications from 25 Oct 2023 for travel from 15 Nov 2023), and the other GCC states and Jordan followed from Feb 2024 — [Wikipedia: ETA (UK)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Travel_Authorisation_(United_Kingdom)); [Home Office factsheet, Feb 2024](https://homeofficemedia.blog.gov.uk/2024/02/01/electronic-travel-authorisation-eta-scheme-factsheet-february-2024/); [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-kingdom-electronic-travel-authorisation-requirement-forthcoming-for-nationals-of-bahrain-kuwait-jordan-oman-saudi-arabia-and-the-united-arab-emirates.html); [Etihad Hub](https://www.etihadhub.com/en-ae/news/uk-electronic-travel-authorisation-scheme-has-opened-to-gcc-and-jordanian-nationals)
- The fee is £20 per applicant, up from £16 on 8 April 2026. Older guides quoting £10 or £16 are out of date — [VisaVerge](https://www.visaverge.com/visa/uk-electronic-travel-authorisation-how-to-apply-and-what-to-know/); [studyimmigration.com](https://studyimmigration.com/blog/uk-eta-guide-visitors/); [oravisa.com](https://oravisa.com/blog/uk-eta-uae-residents-guide/)
- The ETA is valid for 2 years or until the passport expires, with multiple visits of up to 6 months each — [davidsonmorris.com](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/eta-uk/); [VisaVerge](https://www.visaverge.com/visa/uk-electronic-travel-authorisation-how-to-apply-and-what-to-know/)
- Most decisions are fast. A small proportion need manual review, taking up to 3 working days — [application-eta.uk (commercial site; caution)](https://application-eta.uk/uk-eta-for-arab-gulf-countries-2026/); [oravisa.com](https://oravisa.com/blog/uk-eta-uae-residents-guide/)
- Full enforcement for all visa-exempt nationals (including GCC) started 25 February 2026 — [jobbatical.com](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/uk-electronic-travel-authorisation-eta-2026); [application-eta.uk](https://application-eta.uk/uk-eta-for-arab-gulf-countries-2026/)
- Applications go through the official UK ETA app or GOV.UK. Third-party sites may charge more. The ETA is reportedly also required for passengers transiting the UK — [VisaVerge](https://www.visaverge.com/visa/uk-electronic-travel-authorisation-how-to-apply-and-what-to-know/); [ops.group](https://ops.group/blog/uk-eta-bizav-guide/)
- **Jordan:** Jordanian nationals were moved to a visa requirement in 2024. ## 10. Special rules for Gulf travellers: residence-permit concessions, passport validity, insurance, and the Egypt/Jordan examples

### Takeaway
Residence-based concessions matter mostly for non-GCC nationals living in the Gulf:
- **Armenia:** GCC residence permit holders are visa-free (temporary, reportedly to July 2027).
- **Azerbaijan:** 30-day visa on arrival for permits valid 6+ months (reported).
- **Georgia:** a GCC permit valid 1+ year qualifies for 17 listed nationalities.
- **Japan:** the eVISA is open to UAE residents of non-GCC nationalities.
- **Türkiye:** GCC residence does not satisfy the Egyptian e-visa condition.

Explicit validity and insurance rules found: Uzbekistan requires UAE passports valid at least 6 months, and Georgia requires health insurance.

- Egyptian and Jordanian nationals living in the Gulf should treat Armenia (and possibly Azerbaijan and Georgia) as the residence-friendly options.
